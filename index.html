01 / About

Physics across
different spacetimes.

I am a postdoctoral researcher with the Universe+ project at the University of Amsterdam. My research uses ideas from scattering amplitudes to understand quantum field theory in (Anti-)de Sitter space, particularly cosmological correlators.

Previously, I completed my Ph.D. in Mathematical and Theoretical Particle Physics at Durham University, supervised by Arthur Lipstein.

02 / Research

Questions I work on.

At the interface of high energy theory and cosmology.

01
⟨ · ⟩

Cosmological correlators

Understanding the analytic structure of observables in de Sitter space, and how flat-space scattering amplitudes help organize them.

02
∂ / ∂

Amplitudes & bootstrap

Using symmetry, factorization, and on-shell methods to construct amplitudes and correlators for spinning particles.

03
AdS ↔ dS

Curved spacetime QFT

Studying links between anti-de Sitter and de Sitter physics, including soft limits, recursion, and loop-level structures.
