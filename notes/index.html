Why these notes

The setup matters.

In my research, I have found that an AI assistant can miss essential context when it relies on general online explanations alone. A calculation may depend on the exact observable, helicity convention, gauge choice, or Ward identity used in a particular project.

I keep these self-contained notes to make that context explicit for both human and AI readers. They guide the setup and record what needs checking; they do not replace verification of a derivation.
